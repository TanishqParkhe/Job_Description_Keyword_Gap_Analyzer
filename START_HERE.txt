BHAVYA AI RESUME ANALYZER — TEAM EDITION

FIRST TIME ON A LAPTOP
1. Extract the complete ZIP.
2. Double-click setup_windows.bat.
3. Choose whether to install the optional AI coach.
4. Wait for "Setup complete".

EVERY LATER TIME
Double-click run_app.bat.

CHECK THE INSTALLATION
Double-click verify_setup.bat.

STOP THE APP
Press Ctrl + C in the terminal window.

Do not copy .venv from another laptop. setup_windows.bat creates the correct environment automatically.
